The Story

A rare blend of
strategy, creativity,
and visual mastery.

"Great healthcare marketing doesn't just inform — it inspires trust, builds loyalty, and transforms how people see their health."

Mohammad Alhaj Younes is a seasoned Healthcare Marketing Manager and Brand Strategist with over 18 years of progressive experience across the UAE's leading healthcare institutions. His career spans strategic marketing leadership, medical branding, corporate communications, and event management.

As an Art Director and Professional Photographer, Mohammad brings an extraordinary creative eye to every project — whether developing a hospital's visual identity, directing a multi-platform awareness campaign, or capturing executive portraits and events through his lens.

His unique ability to operate simultaneously at the strategic and creative levels makes him a singular force in UAE healthcare communications. Where most professionals excel in one lane, Mohammad navigates all three — strategy, design, and photography — with equal mastery.

Born from a deep belief that effective healthcare marketing must be both scientifically credible and emotionally resonant, his work consistently bridges the gap between clinical trust and human connection.
